## Black_Friday_Sales_Data_Analysis
Hey there! 👋

🛍️This project was my deep dive into consumer behavior during one of the biggest shopping events of the year — Black Friday. I wanted to uncover how different age groups, genders, occupations, and city types influence buying patterns, and how various brands perform among these segments.

💡 Why I Did This:

1. To explore how sales performance varies across demographics and product categories
2. To strengthen my data analysis and visualization skills with a real-world retail dataset
3. To practice storytelling with data and generate actionable insights for potential business decisions

🛠️ What I Did:

1. Cleaned and preprocessed the dataset to handle missing values and prepare it for analysis
2. Performed detailed Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) to understand distributions, trends, and consumer preferences
3. Used segmentation techniques to compare sales performance across gender, age, city category, occupation, etc.
4. Created clear and insightful visualizations using Matplotlib to support data-driven observations
5. Discovered top-selling product categories, customer segments with highest purchase power, and brand preferences

📌 Key Takeaways:
1. Younger age groups and males showed higher spending patterns
2. Urban cities had better sales performance compared to Tier-2/Tier-3 cities
3. Some brands had strong loyalty in specific age or occupation groups, while others performed steadily across all
4. And lastly i gained extensive experience working on data exploration.

🧰 Tech Stack:
1. Python (Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn)
2.Jupyter Notebook for step-by-step analysis and storytelling
